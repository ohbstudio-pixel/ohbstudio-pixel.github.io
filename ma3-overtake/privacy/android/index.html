Privacy Policy (Android)

Ma3 Overtake · OHB Studio
Effective date: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android version of the Ma3 Overtake app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, and who it is shared with.

The short version

  • You sign in with Google Play Games, or play as a guest. We do not ask you for an email address or a password.
  • Your coins, vehicles, upgrades and match results are saved to your game account on Unity Gaming Services so they cannot be lost or cheated.
  • The app shows adverts through Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads, which use your device's advertising ID.
  • If you agree, we collect anonymous gameplay analytics with Unity Analytics, and adverts may be personalised. You can change this at any time.
  • You can delete your account and data from inside the app.

Your game account

When you sign in with Google Play Games, Google confirms your identity to Unity Gaming Services, which creates a game account for you identified by a random player ID. If you play as a guest, the account is tied to the app on this device instead. Your Play Games gamer name may be shown in the app.

Your game account stores your coins, the vehicles and parts you own, your vehicle setups, your match results and statistics, and how many reward adverts you have watched today. This data is kept so your progress follows you between devices and so the game can check that coins are earned fairly. We do not store your email address, contacts or payment details.

A copy of your progress and your settings is also kept on your device so the game can start without an internet connection.

Age question

The first time you open the app, it asks for your year of birth. Only whether you are under 13 is kept, and only on your device. Players under 13 get no analytics and only adverts suitable for children, as described below.

Analytics

If you are 13 or over and agree, the app sends gameplay events to Unity Analytics: for example which screens you open, which vehicles and parts you look at or buy, how matches end and which reward adverts you watch. Unity also records basic device information (model, operating system, language, country derived from your IP address) and app performance and crash information. We use this to balance prices, fix problems and improve the game. We do not use analytics to identify you personally.

If you say no, or later turn the setting off, analytics stops and we ask Unity to delete the analytics data already collected from you.

Advertising

The app is free and shows banner adverts on the home screen, occasional full-screen adverts between matches, and optional reward adverts you can choose to watch for extra coins. Adverts are served by Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads.

To show and measure adverts and to prevent advertising fraud, these services may collect your device's advertising ID, device model and operating system, language, IP address and approximate location derived from it, and how adverts are viewed and clicked. If you agree to personalised ads, they may use this to show adverts based on your interests. If you say no, adverts are not personalised.

For players under 13, the app tells the ad services that the user is a child under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), so they must not show interest-based adverts or build advertising profiles.

Google Play Games

Signing in with Google Play Games is handled by Google. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Ma3 Overtake is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond what is needed to save their game progress and show child-appropriate adverts. The app has no chat and no way for players to share personal information with each other. If you believe a child's personal information has been collected through the app, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Turn analytics and personalised ads on or off on the Account & data screen (the account button on the home screen).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Sign out of your game account at any time from Account & data.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, open the Account & data screen (the account button on the home screen) and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your game account and the progress stored with it (coins, vehicles, upgrades, match results), asks Unity to delete your analytics data, and removes the copy saved on your device. This cannot be undone.

If you can no longer open the app, email us from the address below and tell us the Play Games gamer name you play under. We will delete your data within 30 days. Data held by Google for your Play Games profile is managed in your Google account.

Security and retention

Your game account data is kept for as long as your account exists and is deleted when you delete your account. Analytics data is kept by Unity according to its own retention policy and is deleted on request as described above. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
