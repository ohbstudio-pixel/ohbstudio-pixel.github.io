Privacy Policy (Android)

Who Knows Kenya: County Quiz · OHB Studio
Effective date: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Android version of the Who Knows Kenya app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, and who it is shared with.

The short version

  • We do not run any servers and we do not ask you to create an account with us.
  • Your progress, scores and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app shows adverts through Appodeal and its ad partners, who may collect device information as described below. Adverts are configured as child-directed.
  • Sign-in to Google Play Games is optional and handled by Google.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress, scores, achievements, unlocked themes, settings and a random install identifier in a file on your device. This identifier is generated by the app, is not linked to your identity, and is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising

The app is free and shows adverts. Buying any banknote theme removes all adverts. Adverts are served by Appodeal, which works with the following advertising partners:

BidMachine, AppLovin, Unity Ads, Liftoff (Vungle), Mintegral, Bigo Ads, InMobi, Chartboost, Moloco, Smaato, Start.io, Verve, PubMatic, Digital Turbine (DT Exchange) and MobileFuse.

To show and measure adverts, these companies may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, device model and operating system, language, IP address and approximate location derived from it, and information about how adverts are viewed and clicked. This information is collected directly by them under their own privacy policies, which you can find on their websites or through Appodeal's privacy policy.

Because the app may be used by children, we have configured our advertising account as child-directed under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), with mature content filtered. This tells ad partners not to show personalised (interest-based) adverts or build advertising profiles. Google's AdMob is not used.

Google Play Games

The app can sign you in to Google Play Games to record achievements and leaderboard scores. This is optional, and the game works fully without it. When signed in, your scores and achievements are sent to Google and may be visible to other players under your gamer profile. We receive only what is needed to display them in the app and do not store it. See the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Banknote themes are sold through Google Play Billing. Your payment information is handled entirely by Google. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made so we can unlock it and restore it if you reinstall.

Local (LAN) multiplayer

Local multiplayer connects devices on the same Wi-Fi network directly to each other. The display name you enter and your game answers are shared only with the other players in that game. Nothing is sent to us or stored on the internet.

Children

Who Knows Kenya is an educational game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no chat, no account sign-up of its own, and no way for players to share personal information with strangers over the internet. Advertising is child-directed as described above. If you believe a child's personal information has been collected through the app, contact us and we will help you remove it.

Your choices

  • Buy any banknote theme to remove all adverts.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Sign out of Google Play Games at any time.
  • Uninstall the app to delete all data stored on your device.

Security and retention

We do not hold any of your data on our own systems. Data held by Google, Appodeal and its partners is kept according to their own policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
