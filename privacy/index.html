Privacy Policy

Who Knows Kenya: County Quiz · OHB Studio
Effective date: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Who Knows Kenya app for iPhone and iPad ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, and who it is shared with.

The short version

  • We do not run any servers and we do not ask you to create an account with us.
  • Your progress, scores and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app does not show adverts, does not use the advertising identifier and does not track you.
  • Sign-in to Game Center is optional and handled by Apple.
  • Purchases are processed by the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress, scores, achievements, unlocked themes, settings and a random install identifier in a file on your device. This identifier is generated by the app, is not linked to your identity, and is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Game Center

The app can sign you in to Game Center to record achievements and leaderboard scores. This is optional, and the game works fully without it. When signed in, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple and may be visible to other players under your Game Center profile. We receive only what is needed to display them in the app and do not store it. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Banknote themes are sold through the App Store. Your payment information is handled entirely by Apple. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made so we can unlock it and restore it if you reinstall.

Local (LAN) multiplayer

Local multiplayer connects devices on the same Wi-Fi network directly to each other. The display name you enter and your game answers are shared only with the other players in that game. Nothing is sent to us or stored on the internet.

Children

Who Knows Kenya is an educational game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no chat, no adverts, no account sign-up of its own, and no way for players to share personal information with strangers over the internet. If you believe a child's personal information has been collected through the app, contact us and we will help you remove it.

Your choices

  • Sign out of Game Center at any time.
  • Uninstall the app to delete all data stored on your device.

Security and retention

We do not hold any of your data on our own systems. Data held by Apple is kept according to its own policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
